The surging demand for AI compute power creates a significant investment and development opportunity in advanced energy infrastructure, particularly nuclear and next-gen power solutions.
Thesis
AI's exponential growth requires unprecedented amounts of reliable, high-density energy, making traditional data center power solutions insufficient. This creates a critical need for new energy generation and distribution technologies capable of supporting 'AI compute campuses,' driving innovation and investment in areas like small modular reactors (SMRs), advanced grid management, and sustainable cooling solutions.
Potential implications
Over the next 1-5 years, we will see accelerated R&D and deployment in nuclear energy, potentially including micro-reactors, alongside significant investment in grid modernization and energy storage. Companies that can provide efficient, scalable, and environmentally sound power solutions for AI infrastructure will gain a substantial competitive advantage. This shift will also create opportunities for specialized engineering, construction, and operational services.
Recommended action
Investigate emerging nuclear energy technologies (e.g., SMRs) and advanced grid solutions, and explore partnerships or ventures focused on developing energy infrastructure specifically for high-density AI data centers.
