III Thematic Watch
Opportunity radar
AI Business Model Shift Horizon · 1–5 yrs
Develop proprietary, logged-in AI-powered services to counter diminishing search traffic and build defensible data moats.
Thesis
As AI search models directly answer user queries, traditional content and service providers are experiencing significant drops in referral traffic and advertising revenue. This forces a strategic pivot towards direct user engagement and the creation of unique, value-added experiences that necessitate a logged-in state. Businesses that can successfully build these 'walled gardens' will control their user data, monetize directly, and create defensible positions against generic AI summarization.
Potential implications
Over the next 1-5 years, the internet will see a bifurcation: generic information will be increasingly commoditized by AI, while specialized, interactive, or community-driven content and services will thrive behind login walls. This shift will favor subscription models, personalized experiences, and platforms that can leverage proprietary user data to enhance their AI offerings. Companies that fail to adapt will struggle with declining traffic and monetization. This creates a strong incentive for entrepreneurs to build niche, high-value platforms with strong community features or unique data sets.
Recommended action
Evaluate existing business models for reliance on third-party search traffic; identify opportunities to create unique, AI-enhanced features or content that require user login, fostering direct relationships and building a proprietary data asset.
