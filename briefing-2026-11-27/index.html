Strategic Reallocation Horizon · 1–5 yrs
Geopolitical tech fragmentation and concentrated AI investment create opportunities in diversified supply chains and overlooked non-AI sectors.
Thesis
The escalating US-China tech rivalry, marked by chip curbs and export controls, is forcing a fragmentation of global tech supply chains, creating an urgent need for diversified sourcing and manufacturing. Concurrently, the intense focus of venture capital on AI is leading to a significant undervaluation of high-quality, non-AI tech companies. This dual dynamic presents a unique opportunity to invest in and build businesses that address supply chain resilience outside traditional hubs, or to acquire/fund profitable tech ventures in overlooked sectors.
Potential implications
Over the next 1-5 years, this trend will likely lead to the rise of new manufacturing and software development hubs in regions less impacted by geopolitical tensions, such as India. Companies that can effectively bridge gaps in diversified supply chains for critical components or offer compelling value in non-AI software and hardware will gain significant competitive advantage. India, seen as a 'safe haven' for investment, is particularly well-positioned to attract capital and talent in these areas.
Recommended action
Explore partnerships or direct investments in alternative semiconductor and component manufacturing, or software development firms, in regions like India or Southeast Asia. Additionally, research and identify profitable, bootstrapped software or hardware companies in non-AI verticals that may be overlooked by AI-focused VCs, presenting potential acquisition or strategic investment targets.
